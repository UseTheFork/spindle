# Aggregate review scope

Read for a comprehensive aggregate review before inspecting changes.

Cover the entire spec and cross-task integration, including omitted required behavior and parked findings. Read relevant prior findings and controller rulings; preserve stable finding IDs and distinguish ruled findings from new regressions. Apply comprehensive Spec and Quality assessment, including correctness, tests, maintainability, and material behavior-preserving simplifications. Accepted individual tickets do not establish whole-spec coverage.

**Complete when:** the report accounts for the whole assigned delivery, cross-task risks, relevant prior rulings, and any coverage limits. Return to the agent's normal report contract; acceptance remains controller-owned.
