# Checkpoint workflow records before delivery evidence

- **Reporter:** User
- **Category:** enhancement
- **Author:** Copilot

## Requirements

After all tickets are complete, SDD shall checkpoint accumulated canonical workflow records at delivery claim, before recording feature-level checks or review. It shall not create an empty commit when there are no pending records. Additional delivery and finalization records may be committed together at finalization. Checkpoints shall preserve the relationship between recorded evidence and its committed code and requirements.

## Acceptance Criteria

- When all tickets are complete and delivery is claimed, the system shall commit accumulated canonical records before any feature-level check or review is recorded.
- When there are no pending records at delivery claim, the system shall not create an empty commit.
- When a checkpoint is interrupted and can be safely resumed, the system shall recover without losing records or duplicating a commit.
- Delivery checks and reviews shall retain their relationship to the committed code and authoritative requirements they assess.
- When finalization adds further workflow records, the system may commit those records at finalization without committing each individual update.
