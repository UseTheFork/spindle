# Document batched workflow record commits

- **Reporter:** User
- **Category:** enhancement
- **Author:** Copilot

## Requirements

SDD guidance shall describe canonical records shared from the feature worktree, uncommitted workflow bookkeeping during ticket execution, and automatic record checkpoints before feature-level delivery evidence. It shall no longer require a commit after each update or a blanket-clean feature worktree. Record the selected trade-offs in a system ADR that builds on the accepted isolated-worktree decision.

- Guidance shall explain canonical record resolution from ticket worktrees, permitted pending bookkeeping, and the delivery checkpoint.
- The Subagent-Driven Development glossary shall define the relevant record terms without implementation details.
- A new ADR shall describe the decision, trade-offs, and relationship to ADR-0011.
- The rendered ticket-tracker guide shall match its source template.
