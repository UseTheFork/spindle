# Record approved T-0004 corrective task-plan exception

- **Reporter:** User
- **Category:** enhancement
- **Author:** Copilot

## Requirements

Add a narrowly scoped, auditable SDD path for the user's approved exception to
the task-plan gate on the already-integrated T-0004 in feature
`0001-ticket-task-dispatch`. The path shall record the approval, its controller,
time, exact ticket, and one-correction scope, and shall create a fresh corrective
assignment without inventing tasks or findings.

The exception shall be usable only for T-0004 in this feature, only once, and
only while its previously integrated implementation is the current state. It
shall not weaken task-plan requirements for any other ticket or permit ordinary
implementation submission without accepted tasks. It shall preserve the
existing T-0004 reports, review assignments/results, findings, check records,
integration, and post-merge evidence as history.

The reopened T-0004 correction shall still require a fresh implementation report,
the existing ticket checks, an independent focused corrective review, a new
integration, and passing post-merge checks before closure. No existing evidence
or gate may be treated as satisfying a gate for the corrective commit.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A controller can invoke an explicit SDD operation that atomically records the
  approved T-0004-only, one-correction exception and opens a corrective
  assignment with a clear objective.
- The exception record is attributable and auditable, identifies its exact
  feature and ticket, and cannot be reused or applied to another ticket or
  feature.
- The operation rejects other tickets, repeated use, missing approval context,
  invalid ticket lifecycle states, and attempts to bypass the task gate for
  ordinary implementation.
- T-0004 remains taskless; the workflow does not create placeholder tasks or
  mark nonexistent tasks accepted.
- Prior assignment, implementation and correction reports, check records,
  review history, findings, integration, and post-merge evidence remain
  inspectable and unchanged as historical evidence.
- The correction must satisfy fresh ticket checks, independent corrective
  review, integration, and passing post-merge validation before T-0004 can
  close.
- Normal task-plan requirements and other ticket completion gates remain
  unchanged.
